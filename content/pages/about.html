What we do

You give us a registration. A member of our team researches that vehicle against the sources we are licensed to use, writes up what the records say, and emails you the report [vehicle_report_sla]. One report, one price: [vehicle_report_price].

Why it is done by a person

An automated lookup returns whatever it has and leaves you to guess at the gaps. We would rather tell you which checks were possible for your vehicle and which were not, and say which is which. That takes longer, and it is the point of the service.

What we are not

  • We are not the DVLA, the DVSA or any other government body, and we are not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of one.
  • We are not a lead generator. We do not sell your details, contact sellers on your behalf, or introduce you to anyone.
  • We are not an instant check, and we do not claim a check we cannot carry out.
