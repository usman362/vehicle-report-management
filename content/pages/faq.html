How quickly will I get my report?

[vehicle_report_sla]. You get an email as soon as it is ready.

Is this an instant check?

No. A person researches each vehicle and writes the report. That is slower than an automated lookup, and it is why the report says what could not be checked as well as what could.

Where do I find the registration?

It is the number on the number plate, and on the V5C logbook. Older, personalised and cherished marks are all fine.

Do I need an account?

No. You order as a guest and we email the report to the address you give at checkout.

Can I share my report link?

Please do not. Anyone who opens the link can read the report. It expires, and we can revoke it and issue a new one if you ask.

What do you do with my data?

We use your email to deliver the report and to support your order. We do not add you to marketing, and we do not pass your details to anyone selling to you. See the privacy policy.
