How it works

You order

Enter the registration, pay securely in pounds, and we confirm your order by email straight away.

We research it

A member of our team checks the vehicle against the sources we are licensed to use, and records which checks were possible.

You receive it

We email a private link to your PDF report [vehicle_report_sla]. The link is yours alone and expires.

What we do not do

  • We do not claim any partnership with the DVLA, the DVSA or any government body.
  • We do not promise checks we cannot actually carry out for your vehicle.
  • We do not generate reports automatically, and we do not guess at missing data.
  • We do not sell, share or market to your details.
