This page explains the cookies this website sets.

Cookies we need

To take an order at all, the site has to remember the registration you entered and what is in your basket while you move between pages, and it has to keep your session secure through checkout. WooCommerce and WordPress set cookies for exactly that. They are set because you are using the service, and the site cannot take an order without them.

If you sign in to the administration area, WordPress sets further cookies to keep you signed in.

Cookies we do not set

We do not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not build a profile of you for marketing.

Your choices

Your browser can block or delete cookies for this site. Blocking the ones listed above will stop checkout working, because the site will no longer be able to keep your order together.

For the operator: this page describes only the cookies the current build actually sets. Adding analytics, a chat widget, remarketing or an embedded video adds cookies that are not strictly necessary, and those need a consent banner and a named entry here before they go on the site.
